## For HR — the three things they need to hear

**1. The bar works, and it is barely binding.** The score floor of 60 — a number *we invented*, which
SFU has never ratified — rejects **two job descriptions out of 874**. Nearly eight in ten current SFU
JDs would pass straight through. **Recommend ratification.**

**2. The bar HR thinks it is approving is not the bar that operates.** Of the 187 current JDs still
blocked: **134 are blocked by a 100–150 word count** on the Position Summary. The *quality score*
blocks 2. **HR believes it is ratifying a quality bar; it is ratifying a word-count range.** (The one
saving grace: that range is SFU's own published number, not ours.)

**3. Almost every alarming number about the archive is an artefact of a calendar, not quality.** A
blocking gate keyed to the territorial-acknowledgement footer is effectively a **date detector** —
the acknowledgement is a rollout still in progress (0% of JDs in 2018 → 11% in 2023 → **89% in
2026**). It blocks 94% of the archive. Old JDs are not bad JDs; they are JDs written before the
paragraph existed. **Recommend the composer auto-inserts the boilerplate rather than penalising
authors for its absence.**

**The full HR packet is [`docs/decisions/HR-REVIEW-PACKET.md`](../decisions/HR-REVIEW-PACKET.md)** —
six decisions, written for a non-engineer, each with measured impact and a recommendation.
**Nothing has been ratified. All 123 decisions remain `open`.**

## For the team — what actually shipped

### 2.5 · The archive baseline (PR #19)
`make baseline` runs the shipped validator + gate runner over the whole archive (~9 min,
single-process, archive bound read-only) and emits segmented data stamped with the rulebook that
produced it. Deliverable: [`docs/baseline/README.md`](../baseline/README.md).

### 2.6 · Three defects in our own rules (PR #20)
Found *by* the baseline. Fixed and re-measured **before** the HR packet went out — handing HR figures
we already knew were wrong, collecting ratification, and *then* correcting them would have made the
sign-off meaningless.

- **`SFU-STRUCT-HOW-WHY` could never *not* fire.** It counted duties missing a `how_why` field our
  parser **never populates**. It fired on **100% of the JDs we would approve** — zero discriminating
  power, a constant subtracted from every score. *Faithful port from hris, where an LLM filled that
  field; our regex parser structurally cannot.* Retired as data; Phase 4 reinstates it with one YAML
  word.
- **`SFU-QUAL-BANNED-PHRASE` scanned the whole document** though its rule text says *Qualifications
  only* — so *"Responsibilities may include arranging catering…"* in **duties** prose tripped a
  Qualifications gate. It drove **all 104** blocks on that gate. **This one fix is the entire
  71.9% → 78.6% gain** (+59 approvals).
- **Our era model conflated two rollouts** four years apart (the JDFN *template*, 2019; the *footer*,
  2023–24), making correctly-authored 2019 JDs un-approvable. A **7× distortion**.

### 3.1 · Tier-1 dedup + a schema correction (PR #21)
`source_documents.sha256` was `UNIQUE` and ingest silently returned the existing row on a duplicate —
so ~**1,972 files would have been ingested with their filenames discarded entirely**, while
`DedupEdge` sat in the schema as dead code. **Now one row per file; dedup is a *finding* (edges), not
a silent collapse.** All three tiers write into one edge table.

> **The finding that pays for Phase 3.5:** **798 of 1,037 duplicate groups (77%) span more than one
> position ID** — 2,463 files. These are **not** re-saves; they are **distinct positions sharing a
> byte-identical JD**. Tier-1 hands clustering a role cluster at similarity **1.0**, free, before a
> single embedding is computed.

## The pattern worth internalising

**Five separate defects this cycle were the same species: code that ran correctly while describing
itself falsely.** Not one broke a test. Not one produced a wrong answer at runtime. Every one would
have shipped a false statement into the document SFU HR signs.

| | The false claim |
|---|---|
| Phase 0 census | *"The footer lives in `word/footer*.xml`"* — **zero** of 20 modern JDs. It's in the body. |
| HR-114 | A **fabricated** "MEASURED" position-ID count matching neither real number. |
| `SFU-STRUCT-HOW-WHY` | A rule that *could not fail*, presented as a quality signal. |
| HR-123 (twice) | *"star grows linearly"* → it degraded to the full clique. Then, introduced **by the fix**: *"a flip only ADDS"* → it wrote mirror pairs the DB constraint provably cannot catch. |
| The baseline itself | *"Verified byte-identical across two runs"* — falsified by a heap address (`<_io.BytesIO object at 0x…>`) leaking into the skip ledger. |

**The orchestrator was caught by this too**, and it is in the handoff: it nearly declared an 81%
footer miss-rate a bug, on the strength of a 20-file sample drawn from the *newest* 400 files — the
one slice where adoption is ~85%.

**The rule, now enforced culturally and in CI:** *every claim about the archive must be checked
against the archive.* A sample of the newest files is not a sample of the corpus. The merge-blocking
reviewer caught all five and stays on the strongest model — it has returned CHANGES REQUIRED on every
task in this project, and **every finding has been real.**
