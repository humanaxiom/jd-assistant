What only HR can decide

In the order they block things. The first two gate everything downstream.

Blocks all

Ratify the approval bar

Every quality rule the system enforces is currently a provisional setting we chose. The full register holds 197 entries, but only about 60 are genuine policy — the rest are technical tuning that shouldn't need HR at all. None has been ratified.

Ask: a working session on the ~60 policy items, grouped into a dozen packages. Until they're signed, the system enforces our guesses consistently — which is useful, but it isn't SFU policy.

Blocks all

Run the pilot: 5–10 roles, one real reviewer

Four JDs have been approved. Nothing about the review experience has been tested by someone who does this work for a living.

Ask: name a reviewer and a date. Everything else we build is guesswork until someone has driven it for an afternoon.

Scope

CUPE — in or out?

The Bank serves 37% of the archive today (APSA / APEX / Polytechnic). CUPE is 31% and is deliberately excluded: it uses a different instrument (the WJQ) with no agreed quality bar, so scoring it against the JDFN template would produce confidently wrong answers.

Ask: should CUPE be in scope? If yes, that's a separate project — HR defines a CUPE quality bar first, then we build to it. The remaining 32% is our problem, not yours: we can't yet reliably tell which group those files belong to, and we're fixing it.

Blocks validation

The valid grade values, per employee group

We read the grade straight out of the document wherever one is stated — 3,049 so far: 2,322 CUPE, 687 APSA, 34 APEX. What we don't have is the authoritative list of what a valid grade is, so we can't tell a correct value from a typo, and grade entry is a free-text box rather than a dropdown.

Ask: the valid grade values for each employee group. A fifteen-minute answer, and the only thing between us and a validated grade field.

Needs a method

Where grade comes from for the rest

11,473 job descriptions state no grade at all. There is no live system of record to pull them from, so this is a question about method, not integration.

Ask: confirm the approach — or tell us the right one.
① HR supplies the valid values, and grade entry becomes a checked dropdown on both the authoring form and the reviewer's screen — both fields already exist. ② The reviewer sets the grade when they approve, which is where the authority already sits. ③ HR spot-checks a sample of the 3,049 we already read — if the sample is right, 21% of the archive arrives already graded. That one needs an afternoon of HR's time and no engineering.

Blocks publishing

Sign off the standard footer

Every generated JD carries SFU boilerplate including the territorial acknowledgement. We must not ship our own wording of that.

Ask: confirm the current official text.

Already fixed

Three problems you flagged were ours

Three rules were mis-scoring the archive. They were our defects, not judgement calls — corrected, and the whole archive re-scored.

So: the numbers in your review packet are the corrected ones. Nothing to decide here.

If something goes wrong

Recovery, in order of likelihood

  • A page asks you to sign in. That is CAS doing its job — sign in with your SFU credentials.
  • A form says "Forbidden". The tab has been open too long. Reload the page and redo the action.
  • A role's title looks like a paragraph. Known: about 4% of titles are still a sentence lifted from the document. Move on — don't open the published role titled "In collaboration with the Communications Officer".
  • The Builder's duplicate panel doesn't appear. It needs a few sentences of content first, and it needs the inference host reachable. Skip it rather than waiting.
  • Anything else. localhost:25800/ready tells you which store is unhappy in one line.
