# SFU gap roadmap

## Goal
Close the gap between the repo’s current JDFN-first JD Bank and the broader SFU HR ecosystem without overreaching before the policy boundaries are defined.

## Phase 1 — Define the actual product boundary

**Timeline:** Immediate  
**Priority:** P0

### Objectives
- confirm that the Builder remains JDFN-only or expand into CUPE/WJQ
- define whether Hay evaluation is advisory or formal
- set project scope and user expectations explicitly

### Deliverables
- scope statement in product docs
- HR decision record for CUPE/WJQ
- HR decision record for Hay authority

### Exit criteria
- all engineering and reviewer teams agree on the product boundary
- the repo docs reflect the approved scope

---

## Phase 2 — Add the first missing native workflow

**Timeline:** Short term  
**Priority:** P1

### Objectives
- support the most obvious SFU site gap: CUPE/WJQ authoring
- add a formal Hay evaluation workflow with transparent labels
- add re-evaluation request management

### Deliverables
- CUPE/WJQ authoring and validation flow
- Hay evaluation record with factor breakdown
- re-evaluation intake and resolution workflow

### Exit criteria
- a non-JDFN role can be authored and reviewed in a governed workflow
- the system distinguishes advisory and official HR outputs clearly

---

## Phase 3 — Add operational HR support

**Timeline:** Medium term  
**Priority:** P2

### Objectives
- support compensation decisions and role-change operations
- ensure traces back to the correct JD revision and reviewer

### Deliverables
- compensation requisition flow
- job-change / reorganization impact tracking
- compensation audit trail

### Exit criteria
- HR can trace every decision to a JD version and reviewer action
- operational processes are governed by the same review model as JD quality

---

## Phase 4 — Consolidate into an end-to-end HR lifecycle

**Timeline:** Longer term  
**Priority:** P3

### Objectives
- unify authoring, validation, evaluation, re-evaluation, and compensation into a single lifecycle
- create a consistent audit and reporting layer

### Deliverables
- lifecycle architecture
- unified permissions model
- reporting / audit dashboard

### Exit criteria
- JD lifecycle is visible end-to-end without leaving the system
- official decision boundaries remain explicit

---

## Recommended sequencing

1. Scope and policy boundary work first
2. CUPE/WJQ authoring and Hay evaluation second
3. Re-evaluation and compensation workflows third
4. Full lifecycle consolidation last

This order minimizes rework and keeps the roadmap aligned with the HR decisions that define what is actually allowed.
